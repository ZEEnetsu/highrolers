# HIGHROLERS — Marketing Agency Website

Redesigned according to the STARMEDIA Marketing Agency Behance design:
https://www.behance.net/gallery/246021465/STARMEDIA-Marketing-Agency-Website-design

## Art Direction & Palette
- **Agency Name**: HIGHROLERS®
- **Color Scheme**: High-contrast black and white palette on a pure white (`#FFFFFF`) background.
- **Graphic Language**: Halftone dithered Earth globe, pixel pegasus, pixel planet with rings, falling silhouettes, 3D star ribbon gift emblem, widescreen halftone crowd wearing 3D glasses, 3 editorial poster cards (layered planes, cross-arrows, isometric dot-matrix cube), executive portraits with scanlines, and pixel dither ribbon dividers.
- **Typography**: Ultra-bold condensed display fonts ('Bebas Neue', 'Anton', 'Syne'), technical monospace ('JetBrains Mono', 'Space Mono') for corner brackets and meta tags, and clean grotesque body ('Work Sans').

## Features & Sections
1. **Technical Topbar**: Live system pulse, bracketed tags (`[ MAIN PAGE ]`, `[ HIGHROLERS ]`, `[ 1920 PX ]`), language toggle (`EN / ES`).
2. **Main Navigation**: Sticky glassmorphism header, brand logo with crosshair glyph, interactive navigation, audio FX toggle, and inverted black pill CTA button.
3. **Hero Section ("MARKETING THAT SELLS")**: Forbes recognition card, rotating 360° circular seal around halftone globe, global reach stats, hashtag badges (`#WEDO THEWORK`), massive display headline, widescreen crowd with 3D glasses, and real-time animated pixel matrix canvas ribbon.
4. **Client Marquee / Partners**: Bracketed partner cards for `aposta.la`, `Lactolanda`, `chicco`, `TREN'`, `LANDERFIT`, `CIABAY`.
5. **How We Do The Work / Philosophy**: Split editorial headline with interactive pixel smiley (`[ CLICK TO CHARGE ]`) and 3 editorial poster cards with pixel art.
6. **Services Accordion**: Pixel pegasus emblem, interactive expandable service drawers with rotating 8-bit arrow badges and pill tags.
7. **Selected Case Studies Matrix**: 4 high-contrast cards with metadata grids (Resumen, Servicios) and modal inspection triggers.
8. **Spotlight Case Study (Chicco)**: Halftone photo, 4 interactive strategic pillars, and 4 giant key metrics (`243% TRAFFIC GROWTH`, `+3496% FOLLOWERS`, `X7 ROI`, `605.4% SALES GROWTH`) with rotating monochrome glyphs.
9. **Interactive ROAS / Growth Simulator**: Dual custom range sliders for monthly ad spend and ROAS with live projected pipeline value and lift calculation.
10. **Fast Conversion Lead Form**: Cyber studio banner, pixel planet and falling figures, clean Swiss input fields, and submit action.
11. **Leadership Team**: 3D star ribbon gift, manifesto, and 4 halftone dithered executive portrait cards with hover scanlines.
12. **Studio Showcase & Pixel Dither Divider**: Highrolers headquarters showcase with pixel transition strip.
13. **Extended Swiss Editorial Footer**: Inverted black grounding base, Google & Meta certified badges, office addresses (Ciudad del Este, Asunción), social links, rotating circular seal, and massive edge-to-edge `HIGHROLERS®` display watermark.
14. **Audio Synthesizer & Modals**: Built-in Web Audio API sound effects, custom crosshair cursor, and case study detail modals.

## Quick Start
1. Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser.
   OR
2. Double-click `start.bat` on Windows.
   OR
3. Run the local python server:
   ```bash
   python serve.py
   ```
   and navigate to `http://localhost:8080/`.
